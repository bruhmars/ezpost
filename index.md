# Privacy Policy — Ezpost

**Last updated:** May 2026

## Who we are

Ezpost (“we”, “the app”) is a personal cross-posting tool operated by me.

## What we collect

- **Account information:** email address and name when you sign up.
- **Linked social accounts:** When you connect to Pinterest or other platforms, we store OAuth tokens (encrypted) needed to read and publish content on your behalf.
- **Activity data:** When you link accounts, enable monitoring, or cross-post content, we log those actions in our database.

## How we use data

- To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
- To detect new photos and videos on accounts you choose to monitor.
- To publish content only to destinations **you** select.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Data storage

Data is stored in a secure database on servers we control. Social tokens are encrypted.

## Your choices

You can disconnect linked accounts and stop monitoring at any time in the app.
